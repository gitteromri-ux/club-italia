Certification

Certified progress. Exam-ready Italian.

Two kinds of certificate matter to our learners: the Club Italia certificate you earn at the end of each course, and the external CILS and CELI examinations that Italian universities and authorities recognise.

Included

Club Italia certificate

Issued at the end of every course, stating the CEFR level reached on the basis of oral and written can-do assessment. A school certificate, signed by eTeacher Group.

Preparation

CILS and CELI

Structured preparation for the two major Italian state-recognised certifications, built into courses from A2 upward and available as focused exam cohorts.

The exams

CILS and CELI, side by side.

CILSCELI
IssuerUniversità per Stranieri di SienaUniversità per Stranieri di Perugia
LevelsA1 to C2A1 to C2
SkillsListening, reading, writing, speakingListening, reading, writing, speaking
Typical useUniversity entry, work, citizenship (B1)University entry, work, citizenship (B1)
WhereAt an authorised exam centre, in Italy or abroad. Club Italia is not an exam centre.

Exam rules, dates and fees are set by the issuing universities and change from year to year. Always check the official CILS and CELI websites before booking.
