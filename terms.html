1. Agreement

By enrolling in a Club Italia course you enter a binding agreement with eTeacher Group Ltd (trading as Club Italia). Please read these terms carefully; they govern payment, attendance, certification, and code of conduct.

2. Enrolment and payment

Course prices are displayed in US dollars, exclusive of any local sales tax that may apply in your jurisdiction.

On enrolment you authorise us to charge the payment schedule you selected: annual single payment, term (two installments), or monthly (recurring).

3. Cooling-off period and refunds

A full refund is available if you request it within seven days of your first live class, no questions asked.

After that period, term and monthly plans are pro-rated to the number of live classes attended; the annual single-payment plan is non-refundable except at our sole discretion in cases of documented hardship.

4. Class attendance and recordings

Every live class is recorded and available in your dashboard for the life of your account. You do not lose access to material by missing a live class.

You may not re-broadcast, publish, resell, or otherwise distribute any recording, teaching material, or platform content outside your personal study.

5. Certification

A CEFR-aligned certificate is issued on successful completion of a full course (typically twenty lessons) at the level you completed, from A1.1 to B1.

The certificate is issued by eTeacher Group and follows the Common European Framework of Reference. It is designed to prepare you for an external, internationally recognised exam such as CILS or CELI at the corresponding level.

We reserve the right to withhold certification for non-completion, plagiarism, or violation of the code of conduct.

6. Code of conduct

Learners are expected to treat teachers and other learners with respect. Harassment, hate speech, discriminatory language, or disruptive behaviour will result in removal from the course without refund.
