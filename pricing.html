What Is Included

Everything that a school should include.

Every price on this page is the full price of the season. There are no textbook fees, no proctor fees, no certificate fees, and no fees to keep access to the recordings once the course is over. All materials are digital, published week by week inside the classroom, and yours to keep.

The one thing we do not include is the placement call, because we do not charge for it. Twenty minutes with an advisor, no obligation, no deposit, no card details. That is the front door of the school and it is open to any adult who wants to speak with us before enrolling.

The Small Print, in Full

Three promises, plainly stated.

i

Cancel any time

Cancel before the next monthly billing date and you owe nothing further. You keep every recording of every class you already attended. Forever.

ii

Change your class

If your teacher or cohort is not the right fit, we move you at no charge. Once, quietly, before the third week of the course.

iii

Placed correctly

If our placement was wrong and you find yourself in a course clearly above or below your level, we credit the difference. That is our error, not yours.
