09.What do I need?

A computer with a webcam, headphones and a stable internet connection. Classes run on Zoom; there is nothing to install beyond that.

010.Is there a refund?

Seven day full refund from your first live lesson.

011.Is Biagio a real teacher?

No. Biagio is an AI conversation tutor trained on our syllabus, included with every course for practice between lessons. Your teacher is a real person.
