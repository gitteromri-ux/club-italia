On price, briefly.

Can I pay in instalments?+
Yes. Annual enrollments may be paid in three equal instalments at zero interest. Term and monthly plans are billed on the same day each month.
Do prices include the CEFR certificate?+
Yes. The printed, signed Club Italia certificate is included at no additional cost on completion of any CEFR track.
Are gift enrollments available?+
Yes. Ask an advisor about gift arrangements; we send a physical enrollment card to your recipient.
Will the price go up?+
The price you enrol at is the price you renew at, for as long as your subscription is continuous.
Can my employer pay?+
Yes. We accept purchase orders and invoicing for employer-funded enrollment. Ask an advisor for the enterprise packet.
