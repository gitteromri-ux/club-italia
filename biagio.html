A conversation, happening this morning.

Biagio · your Italian tutor● online
Come si dice "sold out" in italiano?
Biagio. "Tutto esaurito" — literally "all used up". You'll see it on café signs at the end of a busy Saturday.
E "I'll have another"?
Biagio. "Un altro, per favore." Add a smile and you're a local.
Perfetto, grazie mille.
Biagio. Prego, sempre a disposizione.

What Biagio can do

Six things you can ask Biagio, tonight.

Corrects your Italian

Paste a sentence, an email, a text; Biagio returns a native-register rewrite and explains the change in one line.

Explains grammar in one line

Ask 'why the subjunctive here'; Biagio gives one line of grammar and one line of example. No lecture.

Reads texts aloud

Paste an Italian article; Biagio reads it aloud in a native voice and lets you hit pause on any phrase.

Roleplays a scene

Ask for a market scene, a hotel check-in, a phone reservation. Biagio takes the other role and responds in Italian.

Trained on your syllabus

Biagio knows what you covered in class last Tuesday and won't get ahead of you or fall behind.

Available at every hour

Class is Monday at 7pm. Practice is any hour of any day.

Biagio in the wild

On the train, in the café, on the sofa at midnight.

Biagio lives in your phone the way any good tutor should: quietly, ready, uninterrupting. Ask him for a phrase before your dinner, a correction on an email, a story about Trieste at 2am. He answers in Italian, in English, or in both.
