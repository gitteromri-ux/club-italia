A structured, CEFR aligned curriculum

A curriculum written by specialists in Italian as a foreign language

Action oriented and communicative, aligned with the Council of Europe framework. Every unit states what you will be able to do, then teaches it.

01 Imparare facendo

You act, you speak

Each unit ends in a real task: ordering, asking, booking, explaining. Grammar serves the task.

02 Lingua in contesto

Learned where it is lived

Every unit is set in a real Italian place, taught by a teacher who lives there.

03 La cultura al centro

Culture at the core

Can-do descriptors from A0 to B1, with the art, food and manners that give the words their meaning.

2020Council of Europe, CEFR Companion Volume↗2019Piccardo and North, The Action Oriented Approach↗DITALSUniversità per Stranieri di Siena↗CILSCertificazione di Italiano come Lingua Straniera↗

Club Italia is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Università per Stranieri di Siena. DITALS refers to the individual qualifications held by our teachers. CILS and CELI preparation is offered; examinations are taken at accredited centres.
