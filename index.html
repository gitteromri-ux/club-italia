    Good questions.

    Are the classes live?

    Yes. Every class is taught live by a native teacher in Italy. Recordings are there for review.

    How big is a class?

    Ten to twelve adults. Everyone speaks in every lesson.

    What is included?

    Twenty live lessons of 85 minutes, the syllabus, recordings, Biagio and a Club Italia certificate. A capsule is six sessions of 60 minutes.

    Do you prepare for CILS and CELI?

    Yes, from A2. You sit the exam at an authorised centre of the Università per Stranieri di Siena or di Perugia.

    What does it cost?

    From $62 a week on the annual plan. Full refund within seven days of your first lesson.

    Who are the teachers?

    Native Italians qualified to teach Italian as a foreign language, each in their own city.
