The Method

A curriculum designed by Italian language specialists, not by an app.

01

You act, you speak

Every class is a live communicative event. You speak for at least 40 minutes of every 85. No passive listening, no video watching, no drill homework instead of practice.

02

Learned where it is lived

Every teacher broadcasts from a real Italian city. The light, the traffic, the church bells at noon — the country is in the classroom.

03

Culture at the core

Cinema, opera, cucina, calcio, arte — culture is not a bonus fold, it is the syllabus itself. You learn the language of the people who invented so much of what you already love.

Six Cultural Pillars

Culture speaks Italian. What about you?

Arte & Architettura

From Giotto's Padua to Michelangelo's Rome, the West learned to see through Italian eyes.

Cucina & Vino

Twenty regions, twenty cuisines, four hundred pasta shapes — a language you can taste.

Viaggio & Paesaggio

Rome, Venice, Amalfi, the Dolomites, Sicily — every Italian region is a different Italy.

Cinema & Moda

Fellini, Visconti, Sorrentino, Armani, Prada — Italy dressed and filmed the twentieth century.

Opera & Musica

Verdi, Puccini, Rossini — the operatic art form was born in Italian and is still sung in it.

Tradizione & Storia

Three millennia of history — Etruscan, Roman, Renaissance, Risorgimento — carried in every word.

Common Questions

Everything worth asking before you enroll.

Are the classes really live, or pre-recorded?
Every single class is live, unscripted, and broadcast in real time from Italy. You can see and hear your teacher, your teacher can see and hear you, and your classmates are ten to twelve real people learning with you. Nothing is pre-recorded, ever.
How big are the groups?
Never more than twelve learners, and typically ten. This is the number our academic team has established as the point at which every learner still speaks meaningfully in every class.
What do I need to join?
A laptop or tablet, a stable internet connection, a working microphone, and about ninety minutes a week. Everything else runs in your browser — no downloads, no installs.
Can I watch a class I missed?
Yes. Every live class is recorded and available in your dashboard for the rest of your life — even after you complete the course.
