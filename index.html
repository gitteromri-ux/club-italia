A structured, CEFR-aligned curriculum

A curriculum written by Italian language specialists, against the European framework.

Action-oriented, communicative learning, aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference. You do not study Italian. You act in it.

01

Imparare facendo

You act, you speak

The CEFR treats the learner as a social agent. Every lesson is a task: ordering, booking, negotiating, persuading.

02

Lingua in situ

Learned where it is lived

Every unit is anchored in a real Italian place: a Roman bar, a Florentine workshop, a Bolognese market.

03

La cultura al centro

Culture at the core

Art, food, opera and cinema are the material the language is taught through, with can-do assessment at every milestone.

Your questions, answered

Everything worth asking before you enrol.

01.Are the classes live or pre-recorded?

Every class is live, with a native teacher broadcasting from Italy. Recordings are a revision tool, never a substitute.

02.How big are the groups?

Ten to twelve adult learners. Small enough that everyone speaks in every lesson and the teacher corrects each learner individually.

03.Can I watch classes later?

Yes. Every class is recorded and added to your library, so a missed lesson is never lost.

04.What does a full course include?

Twenty live lessons (six for a cultural capsule), the PDF syllabus, class recordings, Biagio the AI tutor between lessons, and a CEFR-mapped certificate at the end.

05.Do you prepare for CILS and CELI?

Yes. From A2 upward, courses can include exam technique for CILS (Università per Stranieri di Siena) and CELI (Università per Stranieri di Perugia). You sit the exam at an authorised exam centre; Club Italia is not one.

06.How much does it cost?

From $62 a week on the annual plan, $73 a week by term, or $84 a week monthly. Seven day full refund from your first live lesson.

07.Who teaches the classes?

Native Italian teachers qualified to teach Italian as a foreign language, each broadcasting from their own city.
