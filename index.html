The CEFR ladder

From first ciao to full fluency

Every course sits on a defined rung of the Common European Framework. You climb the ladder in order, or you start where you already are.

A0CiaoFirst words, sounds, greetings.
A1.1Prime paroleIntroduce yourself. Talk about your day.
A1.2Vita quotidianaOrder, ask directions, small talk.
A2.1SicuroPast, future, opinions.
A2.2CertificatoIndependent everyday Italian.
B1FluenteSpeak with confidence about work and life.
