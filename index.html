03

The Method

Six ideas we practise, not preach.

The Club Italia method is not a slogan. It is six habits we defend in every class, drawn from twenty-five years of teaching adults to speak.

i

Culture is content

You never study a grammar point in a vacuum. Every rule is a door into a Sicilian kitchen, a Florentine studio, a Milanese boardroom.

ii

Small always

Ten to twelve students in a live room. Enough voices to make a conversation, few enough to know each name.

iii

Native only

Every teacher was born in Italy, trained in Italian pedagogy, and teaches from an Italian city, not a suburb of one.

iv

Speak from day one

No silent months. In lesson one, you leave the room having spoken sentences you needed and understood.

v

Repetition, gently

Spaced practice with Biagio. What is drilled becomes yours. What is only introduced is quietly forgotten.

vi

A season, not a course

Twenty lessons that arc like a novel. First act. Second act. The satisfaction of an ending you earned.

The Questions

What most adults ask first.

How many people are in a live class?
Between 10 and 12. Enough voices to make a real conversation, few enough that a teacher can hear each one every week.
Do I need to know any Italian to begin?
No. Our Principiante and Parliamo Foundation courses start at A0, from your first buongiorno. A placement call will confirm the right room for you.
What if I miss a live class?
Every class you attend is recorded and stays in your library for life. Miss a Tuesday and you can catch it back on a Friday morning.
Is the certificate recognised?
Yes. The Club Italia certificate is CEFR-aligned, follows the Common European Framework criteria, and is issued by eTeacher Group.
Are the teachers really native?
Every teacher was born in Italy, trained in Italian pedagogy, and teaches live from an Italian city. We publish their background pages so you can read who is in the room.
