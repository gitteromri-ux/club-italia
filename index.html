CEFR ladder

From first ciao to full fluency.

Every course sits on a defined CEFR rung. You climb in order, or you start where you already are.

A0CiaoFirst words, sounds, greetings.
A1.1Prime paroleIntroduce yourself and your day.
A1.2Vita quotidianaOrder, ask directions, small talk.
A2.1SicuroPast, future, opinions.
A2.2CertificatoIndependent everyday Italian.
B1FluenteSpeak with fluency about work and life.

FAQ

The questions we hear most.

How is Club Italia different from Duolingo or Rosetta Stone?

You are in a live small-group class with a native Italian teacher broadcasting from Italy. You speak every lesson. No solo tapping on a screen.

What if I already speak some Italian?

Take our free placement call. We place you in the correct CEFR rung so you neither repeat nor drown.

What if I miss a class?

Every class is recorded. You get lifetime access to your library.

How large are the groups?

Ten to twelve learners per group. No exception.

Is there an official certificate?

Yes. eTeacher CEFR-aligned certificate on completion of each course.

Can I get a refund?

Seven-day full refund from your first live lesson. No questions asked.

What times are classes?

Multiple time zones. Morning Italy, evening Italy, evening US East. You choose at enrolment.

Do I need to speak Italian to start?

No. CI Principiante starts at A0 — the sound of the language itself.
