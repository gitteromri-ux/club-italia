The CEFR ladder

From first ciao to full fluency.

Every course sits on a defined rung of the Common European Framework. You climb the ladder in order. Or you start where you already are.

A0CiaoFirst words, sounds, greetings.
A1.1Prime paroleIntroduce yourself. Talk about your day.
A1.2Vita quotidianaOrder, ask directions, small talk.
A2.1SicuroPast, future, opinions.
A2.2CertificatoIndependent everyday Italian.
B1FluenteSpeak with confidence about work and life.

Questions

The questions we hear most.

How is this different from Duolingo or Rosetta Stone?

Those are self-serve apps. Club Italia is a live small-group class with a native teacher broadcasting from Italy. You speak every lesson.

What if I already speak some Italian?

Book a free placement call. We put you on the correct CEFR rung so you neither repeat nor drown.

What if I miss a class?

Every class is recorded. You keep the library for life.

How large are the groups?

Ten to twelve learners. No exception.

Is the certificate official?

Yes. eTeacher-signed, CEFR-aligned. Accepted for citizenship and university applications in Italy.

Can I get a refund?

Seven-day full refund from your first live lesson. No questions.

What times are classes?

Morning Italy, evening Italy and evening US East. You pick at enrolment.

Do I need to speak any Italian to start?

No. CI Principiante starts at A0. The sound of the language itself.
