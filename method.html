Club Italia / Method
Our method

You don't study Italian. You act in it.

An action-oriented, communicative method aligned with the Common European Framework. Every lesson ends with something you can do in Italian that you could not do before.

Enrol nowFind my level

A language is a set of actions: ordering, asking, persuading, consoling, joking. We teach the actions first and the grammar they need, in that order.

The principle
Three principles

The Club Italia method.

01

Imparare facendo

Learning by doing

The CEFR treats the learner as a social agent. Each lesson is a task: ordering, booking, negotiating, persuading.

02

Lingua in situ

Situated in a place

Every unit is anchored in a real Italian city and situation, so the words are the words Italians use there.

03

La cultura al centro

Culture as content

Art, food, opera and cinema are the material the language is taught through, not an extra.

Eighty-five minutes

Inside one lesson.

A typical lesson structure.

0 to 10

Riscaldamento

Warm-up conversation, review of the last can-do.

10 to 25

Input

A real text, clip or scene from the unit city.

25 to 40

Focus on form

The grammar the task needs, on the shared screen.

40 to 65

Task

Pairs and trios in breakout rooms.

65 to 80

Feedback

Personal correction, error by error.

80 to 85

Can-do check

What you can now do, and the Biagio practice for the week.

The weekly cycle

Before, during, after.

Before

Biagio previews the unit vocabulary.

During

Live lesson with your native teacher.

After

Recording, PDF notes, Biagio practice.

Every 10 lessons

A can-do check with written feedback.
