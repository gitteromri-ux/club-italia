Minute by minute

The eighty five minutes,
opened up

Minutes
Segment
What happens
0 to 5
Buongiorno round
Marco greets every student by name. Each student replies in one full Italian sentence about the weekend.
5 to 15
Warm review
Vocabulary from last week returns, in a light quiz on the shared screen. No score. A conversation.
15 to 30
New material
Marco introduces the topic of the day, in Italian, illustrated on the shared screen. English is used sparingly and briefly.
30 to 45
Guided practice
Breakout in pairs. Each pair works through a short conversation script. Marco visits every room.
45 to 55
Break
Ten minutes off camera. Marco stays online. Students who want to stay do.
55 to 75
Free conversation
On the topic of the day. Marco holds the room in Italian. English is not used in this segment.
75 to 85
Review and homework
Marco writes the week's take home task on the shared screen. Questions. Farewells.
Transcript sample

Two minutes of Italian,
with an English gloss

Italian, as spoken

Marco: Allora, Diane, com'è andato il weekend a Portland?

Diane: Bene, grazie. Sono andata al mercato con mia figlia.

Marco: Bello. E cosa avete comprato?

Diane: Del pane, un po' di formaggio, e due bottiglie di vino italiano.

Marco: Vino italiano. Bravissima. Da che regione?

Diane: Non ricordo. Un rosso della Toscana, credo.

English gloss

Marco: So, Diane, how was the weekend in Portland?

Diane: Good, thank you. I went to the market with my daughter.

Marco: Nice. And what did you buy?

Diane: Some bread, a little cheese, and two bottles of Italian wine.

Marco: Italian wine. Well done. From which region?

Diane: I do not remember. A red from Tuscany, I think.
