Frequently Asked

Fifteen questions, honestly answered.

Placement & Enrolment
Do I need to speak any Italian to begin?
Not at all. Our Principiante and Parliamo Foundation courses start at A0, from your first buongiorno. A twenty-minute placement call with an advisor confirms the correct starting point for you.
How is my level assessed?
By talking with you. We do not use a written test. An advisor, or one of our teachers, holds a short conversation in Italian and English to place you at exactly the right level, not one below and not one above.
When can I start?
We open one new cohort per course each month. Our next cohort begins in Ottobre 2026, and the following one in Novembre. Your placement call will confirm the exact start date that fits you.
Can I switch to a different course after I start?
Yes, once, at no charge, before the third week. If a course turns out to be above or below the right level for you, we move you to the right cohort without argument.
The Live Classroom
How many students are in a live class?
Between 10 and 12. Enough voices to make a real conversation, few enough that the teacher can hear each one, every week. When a class fills, we open a new one. We never overbook.
What if I miss a live class?
Every live class you attend is recorded and saved to your library for life. If you miss a Tuesday, you can watch it back before the Friday class and rejoin without falling behind. We do not police attendance.
Are all teachers native Italians?
Yes. Every Club Italia teacher was born in Italy, trained in teaching Italian as a foreign language, and broadcasts from an Italian city. We publish their regional voice and background so you know who is in the room.
What time zones do the classes work in?
We run multiple cohorts of each course, spread across the day, so students in North America, Europe, and Australia can all find an 85-minute class that fits a working adult's calendar.
Certification & Recognition
Is the certificate recognised?
Yes. The Club Italia certificate is aligned to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), which is the standard used by universities, employers, and immigration authorities across Europe.
Can this help with Italian citizenship or a visa?
The CEFR alignment of our courses is the same framework Italian authorities use. For citizenship or visa purposes, we recommend the official Italian state exams (CELI, CILS, PLIDA); a Club Italia season is excellent preparation for them.
Payment & Guarantees
How is billing arranged?
You are billed monthly on the same calendar date, in the currency of your country. There are no long-term contracts and no set-up fees. Cancel any time before the next billing date and nothing further is owed.
Is there a free trial?
We do not run trials. We do offer a free sample class recording (a real ninety-minute session, not a highlight reel) and a twenty-minute placement call, both without payment details. We believe those two together are more useful than a trial.
What is your refund policy?
Cancel any time. You are billed only for the months you have taken. If a technical failure on our side loses you a class, we credit it. If your teacher is unavailable, a Club Italia teacher of the same level covers, or we reschedule.
Biagio, the AI Tutor
Is Biagio a replacement for a teacher?
No. Biagio is a between-classes practice partner. He drills what your teacher taught, corrects your written homework, and holds unlimited conversations in Italian at any hour. He makes the teacher's work stick. He does not do the teacher's work.
Is my conversation with Biagio private?
Yes. Your conversations are private to you. We do not use them to train third-party models, we do not sell them, and we do not share the contents with anyone outside our own pedagogy team, and only in anonymised form, to improve the tutor.
