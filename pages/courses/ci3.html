The DNA of this course

What makes Intermedio different

Three reasons Italy's oldest university city is the right place to graduate from A1.

01

Sotto i portici

Bologna has forty kilometres of covered arcades. Our lessons take you under them — to bookstores, cafés, and the tables where local politics is argued nightly. You learn the language of an argued opinion.

02

Il congiuntivo, con calma

A2 introduces the subjunctive — the tense that most learners fear. We introduce it slowly, through examples of Bolognese cuisine, and by lesson fifteen you will use it without noticing.

03

Una maestra bolognese

Francesca teaches every lesson. She is a sociolinguist by training, which means she can explain, in one sentence, why a phrase you learned in Rome sounds strange in Bologna — and how to bridge the two.

The city is the classroom

Why Bologna teaches the argument

The middle passage of Italian belongs to the city that has been arguing, teaching, and eating since 1088.

Bologna is Italy's classroom. The Alma Mater Studiorum, founded in 1088, made this city a place where people came to learn — and where, over nine hundred years, an entire vocabulary of study, argument, and generous hospitality was polished under the porticoes.

A1.2 to A2.1 is the middle passage — the level where you stop translating in your head and start thinking in Italian. Bologna is the perfect city for that transition, because Bolognesi are precise without being severe. They will finish your sentence for you if you pause, but they will do it kindly, and they will pour you another glass of Sangiovese while they do.

This course lives in the city's three nicknames: la Dotta, the university and its lecture halls; la Grassa, the food markets, tortellini rolled by hand, tagliatelle at Trattoria Anna Maria; and la Rossa, both the terracotta rooftops and the political conversations you will overhear in every osteria after nine at night.

The full curriculum

Twenty live lessons, one journey

A structured A1.2 → A2.1 curriculum, each 85-minute live class themed to the setting of the course.

01

Bologna la dotta

Italy's oldest university, the arcades, the food. Review of A1.2.

02

Passato remoto in the wild

Recognising passato remoto in fairy tales and news. Passive knowledge only.

03

Trapassato prossimo

Talking about what happened before what happened. Storytelling structure.

04

Imperfetto vs passato prossimo

The Italian storytelling engine. Real narratives from a Bolognese childhood.

05

La cucina emiliana

Ragù, tortellini, mortadella, Parmigiano. Cooking verbs and cultural vocabulary.

06

A tavola con gli amici

Dinner-party Italian. Interrupting politely, agreeing, disagreeing, refilling wine.

07

Ho un'opinione

Expressing opinion: penso che, credo che, secondo me. Congiuntivo introduced softly.

08

Il mio quartiere

Comparing places: più / meno / così… come. Bologna vs Milano vs Roma.

09

Andiamo al cinema

Reserving tickets, film genres, describing a plot, recommending.

10

La musica italiana

Lucio Dalla, Battisti, De Andrè, contemporary indie. Listening lab.

11

A metà del cammino

Midpoint oral assessment, cultural presentation.

12

Se dovessi scegliere

Hypothetical in the present: se + congiuntivo imperfetto + condizionale.

13

Le città italiane

Describing Italian cities, geography, north / centre / south differences.

14

Il lavoro in Italia

Vocabulary of work, CV, colloquio di lavoro, contratti.

15

La sanità

Healthcare vocabulary, at the doctor, at the hospital, the Italian public system.

16

La banca

Bank vocabulary, opening an account, paying bills, ordering online.

17

La casa in affitto

Renting an apartment, understanding a contract, negotiating with a landlord.

18

Feste italiane

Italian holidays: Natale, Pasqua, Ferragosto, Palio di Siena, Carnevale di Venezia.

19

Il dialetto

Introduction to Italian dialects — bolognese, romanesco, napoletano, siciliano — as cultural heritage.

20

Arrivederci, Bologna!

Full CEFR A2.1 assessment, oral presentation, path to CI Avanzato.

Listen in

An evening in via del Pratello

A short excerpt from a real lesson. Press listen to hear the pronunciation.

Lesson 12 · Un'osteria in via del Pratello

Nine at night, a wooden table shared with strangers. The waiter, Ruggero, drops off a carafe of red without being asked.

Ruggero
Buonasera. Avete deciso?
Tu
Sì, prendo le tagliatelle al ragù. E vorrei un calice di Sangiovese.
Ruggero
Bene. E per secondo?
Tu
Non ancora. Torno tra dieci minuti.
Ruggero
Come vuole. La cucina chiude alle undici, si ricordi.

Teacher's note. Two conditionals, a future, and a polite imperative — in five turns. This is the density that A2 unlocks.
