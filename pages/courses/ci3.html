What you will be able to do

  • Move between passato prossimo, imperfetto and trapassato with ease
  • Express opinion, doubt and preference clearly

Syllabus

20 units. One clear arc.

  1. 01

    Bologna la dotta

    Italy's oldest university, the arcades, the food. Review of A1.2.

  2. 02

    Passato remoto in the wild

    Recognising passato remoto in fairy tales and news. Passive knowledge only.

  3. 03

    Trapassato prossimo

    Talking about what happened before what happened. Storytelling structure.

  4. 04

    Imperfetto vs passato prossimo

    The Italian storytelling engine. Real narratives from a Bolognese childhood.

  5. 05

    La cucina emiliana

    Ragù, tortellini, mortadella, Parmigiano. Cooking verbs and cultural vocabulary.

  6. 06

    A tavola con gli amici

    Dinner-party Italian. Interrupting politely, agreeing, disagreeing, refilling wine.

  7. 07

    Ho un'opinione

    Expressing opinion: penso che, credo che, secondo me. Congiuntivo introduced softly.

  8. 08

    Il mio quartiere

    Comparing places: più / meno / così… come. Bologna vs Milano vs Roma.

  9. 09

    Andiamo al cinema

    Reserving tickets, film genres, describing a plot, recommending.

  10. 10

    La musica italiana

    Lucio Dalla, Battisti, De Andrè, contemporary indie. Listening lab.

  11. 11

    A metà del cammino

    Midpoint oral assessment, cultural presentation.

  12. 12

    Se dovessi scegliere

    Hypothetical in the present: se + congiuntivo imperfetto + condizionale.

  13. 13

    Le città italiane

    Describing Italian cities, geography, north / centre / south differences.

  14. 14

    Il lavoro in Italia

    Vocabulary of work, CV, colloquio di lavoro, contratti.

  15. 15

    La sanità

    Healthcare vocabulary, at the doctor, at the hospital, the Italian public system.

  16. 16

    La banca

    Bank vocabulary, opening an account, paying bills, ordering online.

  17. 17

    La casa in affitto

    Renting an apartment, understanding a contract, negotiating with a landlord.

  18. 18

    Feste italiane

    Italian holidays: Natale, Pasqua, Ferragosto, Palio di Siena, Carnevale di Venezia.

  19. 19

    Il dialetto

    Introduction to Italian dialects, bolognese, romanesco, napoletano, siciliano, as cultural heritage.

  20. 20

    Arrivederci, Bologna!

    Full CEFR A2.1 assessment, oral presentation, path to CI Avanzato.
