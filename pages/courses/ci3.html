Can-do outcomes

  • Move between passato prossimo, imperfetto and trapassato with ease
  • Express opinion, doubt and preference clearly

  • Understand fast native speech in most everyday situations

  • Take part in a small group conversation for the full 85 minutes
The full curriculum

20 units. One clear arc.

Every lesson has a named objective, a grammar or language focus and a setting. Open a unit to read it.

01Bologna la dottaLesson 1 · 85 min

Italy's oldest university, the arcades, the food. Review of A1.2.

02Passato remoto in the wildLesson 2 · 85 min

Recognising passato remoto in fairy tales and news. Passive knowledge only.

03Trapassato prossimoLesson 3 · 85 min

Talking about what happened before what happened. Storytelling structure.

04Imperfetto vs passato prossimoLesson 4 · 85 min

The Italian storytelling engine. Real narratives from a Bolognese childhood.

05La cucina emilianaLesson 5 · 85 min

Ragù, tortellini, mortadella, Parmigiano. Cooking verbs and cultural vocabulary.

06A tavola con gli amiciLesson 6 · 85 min

Dinner-party Italian. Interrupting politely, agreeing, disagreeing, refilling wine.

07Ho un'opinioneLesson 7 · 85 min

Expressing opinion: penso che, credo che, secondo me. Congiuntivo introduced softly.

08Il mio quartiereLesson 8 · 85 min

Comparing places: più / meno / così… come. Bologna vs Milano vs Roma.

09Andiamo al cinemaLesson 9 · 85 min

Reserving tickets, film genres, describing a plot, recommending.

10La musica italianaLesson 10 · 85 min

Lucio Dalla, Battisti, De Andrè, contemporary indie. Listening lab.

11A metà del camminoLesson 11 · 85 min

Midpoint oral assessment, cultural presentation.

12Se dovessi scegliereLesson 12 · 85 min

Hypothetical in the present: se + congiuntivo imperfetto + condizionale.

13Le città italianeLesson 13 · 85 min

Describing Italian cities, geography, north / centre / south differences.

14Il lavoro in ItaliaLesson 14 · 85 min

Vocabulary of work, CV, colloquio di lavoro, contratti.

15La sanitàLesson 15 · 85 min

Healthcare vocabulary, at the doctor, at the hospital, the Italian public system.

16La bancaLesson 16 · 85 min

Bank vocabulary, opening an account, paying bills, ordering online.

17La casa in affittoLesson 17 · 85 min

Renting an apartment, understanding a contract, negotiating with a landlord.

18Feste italianeLesson 18 · 85 min

Italian holidays: Natale, Pasqua, Ferragosto, Palio di Siena, Carnevale di Venezia.

19Il dialettoLesson 19 · 85 min

Introduction to Italian dialects, bolognese, romanesco, napoletano, siciliano, as cultural heritage.

20Arrivederci, Bologna!Lesson 20 · 85 min

Full CEFR A2.1 assessment, oral presentation, path to CI Avanzato.

Your teacher · Bologna

Giulia Moretti

Native teacher · DITALS II

Giulia is Bolognese on both sides for four generations. She read modern languages at the Università di Bologna and then trained specifically in food language, the specialised Italian of ingredients and preparation and market negotiation. She teaches from the corner of her kitchen. Copper pans hang on the wall behind her. That is the classroom.

«The intermediate stage is the honest stage. The tourist phrases are behind you, the certificate is far ahead, and in the middle is a real language that has to become yours. I teach that middle.»

Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

01.Are the classes live?

Every class is live, taught by a native teacher broadcasting from Italy. Recordings are for revision, never a substitute.

02.How big are the groups?

Ten to twelve adults. Everyone speaks in every lesson and the teacher corrects each learner personally.

03.What does a course include?

Twenty live lessons of 85 minutes (six sessions of 60 minutes for a capsule), the PDF syllabus, recordings, Biagio the AI tutor and a CEFR-mapped Club Italia certificate.

04.Do you prepare for CILS and CELI?

Yes, from A2 upward. You sit the exam at an authorised centre of the Università per Stranieri di Siena (CILS) or di Perugia (CELI). Club Italia is not an exam centre.

05.How much does it cost?

From $62 a week on the annual plan, $73 a week by term, $84 a week monthly. Seven day full refund from your first live lesson.

06.Who teaches?

Native Italian teachers holding the DITALS qualification for teaching Italian as a foreign language, each in their own city.
