What you will learn

By lesson 20, you will…

CEFR target · A1.1

«Can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases, introduce themselves and interact in a simple way if the other person speaks slowly and clearly.»

Global descriptor, paraphrased from the Council of Europe CEFR.

Can-do outcomes

  • Introduce yourself, family and work in Italian
  • Order food, coffee and drinks with confidence
  • Understand slow, clear spoken Italian in daily situations
  • Handle numbers, dates, times and money
  • Write short messages and postcards in Italian
  • Read menus, signs and simple posts
The full curriculum

20 units. One clear arc.

Every lesson has a named objective, a grammar or language focus and a setting. Open a unit to read it.

01Ciao, Roma!Lesson 1 · 85 min

The Italian sound system, greetings, subject pronouns, the verb essere. Set at Fiumicino, first steps in Rome.

02Chi sei?Lesson 2 · 85 min

Introducing yourself, nationalities, avere, definite articles. A dialogue at a Roman café.

03La mia famigliaLesson 3 · 85 min

Family vocabulary, possessive adjectives, plural nouns. Photos from a real Roman family.

04Al barLesson 4 · 85 min

Ordering coffee, present tense of regular verbs (-are), c'è / ci sono. The espresso ritual.

05In cittàLesson 5 · 85 min

Prepositions of place, andare and venire, asking for directions. The seven hills of Rome.

06A che ora?Lesson 6 · 85 min

Telling time, days of the week, months, the verb fare. A day in the life of a Roman.

07A tavolaLesson 7 · 85 min

Food vocabulary, partitives, quanto costa, ordering in a trattoria. Cucina romana.

08Mi piaceLesson 8 · 85 min

Piacere, opinions, comparatives, likes and dislikes. Italian film and music.

09Che tempo fa?Lesson 9 · 85 min

Weather, seasons, dovere / potere / volere. Weekends in the Roman countryside.

10A casaLesson 10 · 85 min

Rooms of the house, furniture, dare and stare. Roman apartment life.

11VestitiLesson 11 · 85 min

Clothes, colours, adjectives that agree, sapere vs conoscere. Via del Corso, the shopping street.

12Al mercatoLesson 12 · 85 min

Food shopping, markets, imperative for polite requests. Campo de' Fiori.

13In ufficioLesson 13 · 85 min

Work vocabulary, jobs, telefonare, scrivere. A day in a Roman office.

14In viaggioLesson 14 · 85 min

Trains, tickets, hotels, the near future with andare a + infinitive. Termini Station.

15A che punto sei?Lesson 15 · 85 min

Review, mini test, oral presentation. Half-way milestone.

16Passato prossimoLesson 16 · 85 min

Introduction to the past tense with avere. Yesterday in Rome.

17Passato prossimo con essereLesson 17 · 85 min

The past with essere, agreement of the past participle. Stories from a Sunday.

18Il mio quartiereLesson 18 · 85 min

Describing places, reflexive verbs, daily routine. Trastevere on a Saturday.

19Il fine settimanaLesson 19 · 85 min

Weekend plans, imperfetto introduced, contrastive with passato. A country outing.

20Arrivederci, Roma!Lesson 20 · 85 min

Full oral CEFR A1.1 assessment, farewell dialogue, next steps. Ready for CI Elementare.

Your teacher · Roma

Marco Rinaldi

Native teacher · DITALS II, Laurea in Linguistica

Marco grew up in the Prati neighbourhood of Rome, five streets from St Peter's, in a family of teachers. He studied linguistics at Sapienza, wrote a thesis on how adults acquire a second phonology, and has taught Italian to adults ever since. He teaches from a study lined with paperbacks and one framed photograph of his grandfather, who was also a Roman teacher.

«I love the first class. I love the moment a person who has never spoken Italian says buongiorno back to me and hears their own voice make a sound they did not make yesterday. That is why I only teach beginners.»

Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

01.Are the classes live?

Every class is live, taught by a native teacher broadcasting from Italy. Recordings are for revision, never a substitute.

02.How big are the groups?

Ten to twelve adults. Everyone speaks in every lesson and the teacher corrects each learner personally.

03.What does a course include?

Twenty live lessons of 85 minutes (six sessions of 60 minutes for a capsule), the PDF syllabus, recordings, Biagio the AI tutor and a CEFR-mapped Club Italia certificate.

04.Do you prepare for CILS and CELI?

Yes, from A2 upward. You sit the exam at an authorised centre of the Università per Stranieri di Siena (CILS) or di Perugia (CELI). Club Italia is not an exam centre.

05.How much does it cost?

From $62 a week on the annual plan, $73 a week by term, $84 a week monthly. Seven day full refund from your first live lesson.

06.Who teaches?

Native Italian teachers holding the DITALS qualification for teaching Italian as a foreign language, each in their own city.
