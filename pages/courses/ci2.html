The Promise

By the end of CI Elementare, you can:

  • Narrate a past event or weekend in detail
  • Describe people, places and things with precision
  • Make and change plans on the phone
  • Handle emails and short letters in Italian
  • Understand a Tuscan waiter, shopkeeper or landlord
  • Read simple newspaper headlines and short articles
The Syllabus · Week by Week

20 lessons, 20 weeks, one continuous arc.

A CEFR-aligned syllabus designed by Italian language specialists. Each lesson is a live 85-minute event set in a specific Italian cultural context.

01
Lesson 01Firenze, culla della lingua
Why Italian is Tuscan. Dante, Boccaccio, Petrarch. Review of A1.1.
02
Lesson 02Ricordi d'estate
Passato prossimo consolidation, time markers (ieri, la settimana scorsa).
03
Lesson 03Da piccolo
Imperfetto for description and habit, comparison with passato prossimo.
04
Lesson 04Le persone
Describing people physically and in character. Adjective agreement in depth.
05
Lesson 05A casa mia
Rooms, prepositions of place, ci per luogo. A Florentine apartment tour.
06
Lesson 06Nella cucina toscana
Regional food vocabulary, ne partitivo, cooking verbs. Ribollita, bistecca, cantucci.
07
Lesson 07Al ristorante
Full dining dialogue, complaining politely, tipping, il conto per favore.
08
Lesson 08Fare la spesa
Weights, containers, packaging. Mercato Centrale.
09
Lesson 09Al telefono
Phone conventions, formal register, leaving a message.
10
Lesson 10Prendo appuntamento
Making appointments — doctor, hairdresser, notary. The Italian bureaucratic register.
11
Lesson 11Il corpo umano
Body vocabulary, at the pharmacy, common ailments, mi fa male + body part.
12
Lesson 12Vestirsi
Reflexive verbs, morning routine, adverbs of frequency.
13
Lesson 13Il tempo libero
Hobbies, sport, Serie A football, Sunday papers.
14
Lesson 14Cinema italiano
Fellini and Sorrentino, film vocabulary, expressing opinion.
15
Lesson 15A metà strada
Midpoint review, oral test, cultural quiz on Florence.
16
Lesson 16Il futuro
Simple future tense, plans, promises, predictions.
17
Lesson 17Se… allora
Introduction to hypothetical: se + present / future, se + imperfetto / condizionale (light touch).
18
Lesson 18Il condizionale
Condizionale for polite requests and wishes: vorrei, mi piacerebbe, potrei.
19
Lesson 19Scrivere una mail
Email conventions, formal vs informal register, greetings and sign-offs.
20
Lesson 20A presto, Firenze!
CEFR A1.2 full assessment, cultural presentation, path to CI Intermedio.
