What you will learn

CEFR target · A1.2

«Can ask and answer simple questions about personal details, where they live, people they know and things they have, and handle numbers, quantities, cost and time.»

Global descriptor, paraphrased from the Council of Europe CEFR.

Can-do outcomes

  • Narrate a past event or weekend in detail
  • Describe people, places and things with precision
  • Make and change plans on the phone
  • Handle emails and short letters in Italian
  • Understand a Tuscan waiter, shopkeeper or landlord
  • Read simple newspaper headlines and short articles
The full curriculum

20 units. One clear arc.

Every lesson has a named objective, a grammar or language focus and a setting. Open a unit to read it.

01Firenze, culla della linguaLesson 1 · 85 min

Why Italian is Tuscan. Dante, Boccaccio, Petrarch. Review of A1.1.

02Ricordi d'estateLesson 2 · 85 min

Passato prossimo consolidation, time markers (ieri, la settimana scorsa).

03Da piccoloLesson 3 · 85 min

Imperfetto for description and habit, comparison with passato prossimo.

04Le personeLesson 4 · 85 min

Describing people physically and in character. Adjective agreement in depth.

05A casa miaLesson 5 · 85 min

Rooms, prepositions of place, ci per luogo. A Florentine apartment tour.

06Nella cucina toscanaLesson 6 · 85 min

Regional food vocabulary, ne partitivo, cooking verbs. Ribollita, bistecca, cantucci.

07Al ristoranteLesson 7 · 85 min

Full dining dialogue, complaining politely, tipping, il conto per favore.

08Fare la spesaLesson 8 · 85 min

Weights, containers, packaging. Mercato Centrale.

09Al telefonoLesson 9 · 85 min

Phone conventions, formal register, leaving a message.

10Prendo appuntamentoLesson 10 · 85 min

Making appointments, doctor, hairdresser, notary. The Italian bureaucratic register.

11Il corpo umanoLesson 11 · 85 min

Body vocabulary, at the pharmacy, common ailments, mi fa male + body part.

12VestirsiLesson 12 · 85 min

Reflexive verbs, morning routine, adverbs of frequency.

13Il tempo liberoLesson 13 · 85 min

Hobbies, sport, Serie A football, Sunday papers.

14Cinema italianoLesson 14 · 85 min

Fellini and Sorrentino, film vocabulary, expressing opinion.

15A metà stradaLesson 15 · 85 min

Midpoint review, oral test, cultural quiz on Florence.

16Il futuroLesson 16 · 85 min

Simple future tense, plans, promises, predictions.

17Se… alloraLesson 17 · 85 min

Introduction to hypothetical: se + present / future, se + imperfetto / condizionale (light touch).

18Il condizionaleLesson 18 · 85 min

Condizionale for polite requests and wishes: vorrei, mi piacerebbe, potrei.

19Scrivere una mailLesson 19 · 85 min

Email conventions, formal vs informal register, greetings and sign-offs.

20A presto, Firenze!Lesson 20 · 85 min

CEFR A1.2 full assessment, cultural presentation, path to CI Intermedio.

Your teacher · Firenze

Chiara Bellini

Native teacher · DITALS II

Chiara was born in Fiesole, above Florence, and grew up walking down the hill to school past cypress rows that appear in half the paintings you have already seen. She read literature at Firenze, taught in Barcelona for two years, then came home. Her Italian is standard Tuscan, the source Italian, the one from which the language on television and in newspapers is drawn. Her English is warm and careful and does not compete with the Italian for space in the class.

«I teach adults. Adults come with a life already written in another language, and my job is not to erase it. My job is to give them a second interior voice, one that speaks Italian back to them when they are washing dishes or driving home.»

Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

01.Are the classes live?

Every class is live, taught by a native teacher broadcasting from Italy. Recordings are for revision, never a substitute.

02.How big are the groups?

Ten to twelve adults. Everyone speaks in every lesson and the teacher corrects each learner personally.

03.What does a course include?

Twenty live lessons of 85 minutes (six sessions of 60 minutes for a capsule), the PDF syllabus, recordings, Biagio the AI tutor and a CEFR-mapped Club Italia certificate.

04.Do you prepare for CILS and CELI?

Yes, from A2 upward. You sit the exam at an authorised centre of the Università per Stranieri di Siena (CILS) or di Perugia (CELI). Club Italia is not an exam centre.

05.How much does it cost?

From $62 a week on the annual plan, $73 a week by term, $84 a week monthly. Seven day full refund from your first live lesson.

06.Who teaches?

Native Italian teachers holding the DITALS qualification for teaching Italian as a foreign language, each in their own city.
