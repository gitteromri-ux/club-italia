The syllabus, week by week

Your twenty lessons.

#TopicGrammar & cultural setting
01Firenze, culla della linguaWhy Italian is Tuscan. Dante, Boccaccio, Petrarch. Review of A1.1.
02Ricordi d'estatePassato prossimo consolidation, time markers (ieri, la settimana scorsa).
03Da piccoloImperfetto for description and habit, comparison with passato prossimo.
04Le personeDescribing people physically and in character. Adjective agreement in depth.
05A casa miaRooms, prepositions of place, ci per luogo. A Florentine apartment tour.
06Nella cucina toscanaRegional food vocabulary, ne partitivo, cooking verbs. Ribollita, bistecca, cantucci.
07Al ristoranteFull dining dialogue, complaining politely, tipping, il conto per favore.
08Fare la spesaWeights, containers, packaging. Mercato Centrale.
09Al telefonoPhone conventions, formal register, leaving a message.
10Prendo appuntamentoMaking appointments — doctor, hairdresser, notary. The Italian bureaucratic register.
11Il corpo umanoBody vocabulary, at the pharmacy, common ailments, mi fa male + body part.
12VestirsiReflexive verbs, morning routine, adverbs of frequency.
13Il tempo liberoHobbies, sport, Serie A football, Sunday papers.
14Cinema italianoFellini and Sorrentino, film vocabulary, expressing opinion.
15A metà stradaMidpoint review, oral test, cultural quiz on Florence.
16Il futuroSimple future tense, plans, promises, predictions.
17Se… alloraIntroduction to hypothetical: se + present / future, se + imperfetto / condizionale (light touch).
18Il condizionaleCondizionale for polite requests and wishes: vorrei, mi piacerebbe, potrei.
19Scrivere una mailEmail conventions, formal vs informal register, greetings and sign-offs.
20A presto, Firenze!CEFR A1.2 full assessment, cultural presentation, path to CI Intermedio.

Common questions

Answer your top questions.

Is this really live?＋
Every session is a live class taught by a native teacher in Italy, at the scheduled hour, in a group of ten to twelve. If you cannot attend live, the class is recorded for you to review the same evening.
What if I have never spoken Italian before?＋
If you are placed in this course by your advisor, it is the right starting point for your level. If you are not sure, book a free placement call and we will listen to your level in ten minutes.
How long is one lesson?＋
Every live lesson is eighty-five minutes. Long enough to enter a real conversation, short enough to remain fully present.
Can I pick my teacher?＋
On annual plans yes — you can request Chiara Belli by name. On monthly and term plans we place you with the best available fit for your level and time zone.
What device do I need?＋
A laptop or desktop with a working camera and a good microphone. Headphones are recommended. Everything runs inside your browser.
What if I miss a class?＋
Every class is recorded. Chiara will send you a fifteen-minute personal note by email so you never fall behind.

Reserve your placement call.

Thirty minutes with an academic advisor. Spoken in English, honest about your level, ending with a clear next step.

We reply within one working day. No sales script, no pressure.
