The culture behind the classroom

Not a language. A way of living.

Every Club Italia session is set inside a piece of Italian daily life. This course is scored to Firenze · its cafés, its markets, its small rituals of politeness. You learn the words the way an Italian learned them: because someone said them to you first.

By the end of Elementare

Five things you will actually be able to do.

Not "gain exposure to". Not "become familiar with". Each of the five outcomes below is assessed live, on video, with a native speaker.

  • 01Move fluently between past, imperfect, future and conditional
  • 02Tell a 5-minute story about your childhood or last trip
  • 03Handle a hotel check-in, a doctor's visit, a shopping negotiation
  • 04Write a formal email in idiomatic Italian
  • 05Read a museum caption in Florence without help

Questions worth asking.

What if I miss a class?+
Every session is recorded and uploaded to your course page within 24 hours. You retain access to your recordings for the life of your enrollment.
Can I switch levels?+
Yes, within your first four weeks, at no cost. Beyond that, we reassess at the start of every new term and transfers remain free.
What technology do I need?+
A laptop or tablet with a camera, a stable internet connection, and a quiet room. If you can join a video call, you can attend Club Italia.
Do you offer a refund?+
Yes. You have seven days from your first live class to request a full refund.
What is the placement call?+
A 20-minute video call with an academic advisor to place you in the correct level. No test, no fee, no obligation.
