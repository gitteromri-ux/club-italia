The Promise

By the end of CI Avanzato, you can:

  • Use congiuntivo in context, not just in tests
  • Follow a fast Italian film without subtitles most of the time
  • Argue, persuade and negotiate in a small-group setting
  • Write structured emails, reviews and short articles
  • Understand regional accents from north to south
  • Sit and pass an internationally recognised A2 exam
The Syllabus · Week by Week

20 lessons, 20 weeks, one continuous arc.

A CEFR-aligned syllabus designed by Italian language specialists. Each lesson is a live 85-minute event set in a specific Italian cultural context.

01
Lesson 01Napoli e Milano — due Italie
The northern-southern axis of Italian identity. Review of A2.1.
02
Lesson 02Congiuntivo presente
Full introduction to the subjunctive: after verbs of opinion, doubt, feeling.
03
Lesson 03Congiuntivo imperfetto
Backshift, hypothetical use, se + imperfetto + condizionale.
04
Lesson 04Discorso indiretto
Reported speech: he said that…, she asked whether…
05
Lesson 05La stampa italiana
Reading Corriere della Sera and la Repubblica. Newspaper Italian.
06
Lesson 06Il cinema italiano
Full film analysis: La Grande Bellezza, La Vita è Bella, Il Postino.
07
Lesson 07Opera in italiano
Understanding a Verdi libretto. Aria listening lab.
08
Lesson 08Business Italian
Meeting vocabulary, presenting, negotiating, closing a deal.
09
Lesson 09La politica italiana
Institutions, parties, elections — the vocabulary of civic life.
10
Lesson 10Metà strada
Full midpoint oral, written and listening assessment.
11
Lesson 11Argomentare
Debating in Italian. Structured arguments, giving and refuting points.
12
Lesson 12Persuadere
The language of sales, marketing and advertising. Rhetorical devices.
13
Lesson 13Napoli — la lingua della strada
Napoletano expressions that entered standard Italian. Neapolitan cinema.
14
Lesson 14Milano — la lingua degli affari
Milanese business register, aperitivo culture, fashion vocabulary.
15
Lesson 15La letteratura contemporanea
Ferrante, Calvino, Baricco — reading passages and discussion.
16
Lesson 16La cucina regionale
Twenty regions, twenty cuisines — a culinary tour of Italy.
17
Lesson 17Il sistema scolastico
Italian education from elementari to università, plus the language of academia.
18
Lesson 18Vivere in Italia
Practical language for moving to Italy: codice fiscale, permesso di soggiorno, sanità.
19
Lesson 19Cittadinanza italiana
The language of jure sanguinis, applications, consular Italian.
20
Lesson 20Arrivederci — a presto, in Italia!
Full CEFR A2.2 assessment, cultural graduation, path to B1.
