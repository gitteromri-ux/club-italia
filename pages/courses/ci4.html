The full curriculum

20 units. One clear arc.

Every lesson has a named objective, a grammar or language focus and a setting. Open a unit to read it.

01Napoli e Milano, due ItalieLesson 1 · 85 min

The northern-southern axis of Italian identity. Review of A2.1.

02Congiuntivo presenteLesson 2 · 85 min

Full introduction to the subjunctive: after verbs of opinion, doubt, feeling.

03Congiuntivo imperfettoLesson 3 · 85 min

Backshift, hypothetical use, se + imperfetto + condizionale.

04Discorso indirettoLesson 4 · 85 min

Reported speech: he said that…, she asked whether…

05La stampa italianaLesson 5 · 85 min

Reading Corriere della Sera and la Repubblica. Newspaper Italian.

06Il cinema italianoLesson 6 · 85 min

Full film analysis: La Grande Bellezza, La Vita è Bella, Il Postino.

07Opera in italianoLesson 7 · 85 min

Understanding a Verdi libretto. Aria listening lab.

08Business ItalianLesson 8 · 85 min

Meeting vocabulary, presenting, negotiating, closing a deal.

09La politica italianaLesson 9 · 85 min

Institutions, parties, elections, the vocabulary of civic life.

10Metà stradaLesson 10 · 85 min

Full midpoint oral, written and listening assessment.

11ArgomentareLesson 11 · 85 min

Debating in Italian. Structured arguments, giving and refuting points.

12PersuadereLesson 12 · 85 min

The language of sales, marketing and advertising. Rhetorical devices.

13Napoli, la lingua della stradaLesson 13 · 85 min

Napoletano expressions that entered standard Italian. Neapolitan cinema.

14Milano, la lingua degli affariLesson 14 · 85 min

Milanese business register, aperitivo culture, fashion vocabulary.

15La letteratura contemporaneaLesson 15 · 85 min

Ferrante, Calvino, Baricco, reading passages and discussion.

16La cucina regionaleLesson 16 · 85 min

Twenty regions, twenty cuisines, a culinary tour of Italy.

17Il sistema scolasticoLesson 17 · 85 min

Italian education from elementari to università, plus the language of academia.

18Vivere in ItaliaLesson 18 · 85 min

Practical language for moving to Italy: codice fiscale, permesso di soggiorno, sanità.

19Cittadinanza italianaLesson 19 · 85 min

The language of jure sanguinis, applications, consular Italian.

20Arrivederci, a presto, in Italia!Lesson 20 · 85 min

Full CEFR A2.2 assessment, cultural graduation, path to B1.

Your teacher · Napoli

Luca De Simone

Native teacher · DITALS II, Laurea in Filologia Italiana

Luca is from the Vomero hill in Naples. He read Italian philology at Federico II with a dissertation on twentieth century Neapolitan writing, then trained as a language teacher in Perugia. He teaches the highest level in our course ladder. His students are often preparing for an official CEFR B1 examination in the twelve months after his course.

«I teach the last stage. My students arrive able to hold a conversation and leave with a language that can carry a professional email, a difficult phone call, a family visit that lasts a week. That transition is the hardest part of the whole course and it is the part I most enjoy.»

Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

01.Are the classes live?

Every class is live, taught by a native teacher broadcasting from Italy. Recordings are for revision, never a substitute.

02.How big are the groups?

Ten to twelve adults. Everyone speaks in every lesson and the teacher corrects each learner personally.

03.What does a course include?

Twenty live lessons of 85 minutes (six sessions of 60 minutes for a capsule), the PDF syllabus, recordings, Biagio the AI tutor and a CEFR-mapped Club Italia certificate.

04.Do you prepare for CILS and CELI?

Yes, from A2 upward. You sit the exam at an authorised centre of the Università per Stranieri di Siena (CILS) or di Perugia (CELI). Club Italia is not an exam centre.

05.How much does it cost?

From $62 a week on the annual plan, $73 a week by term, $84 a week monthly. Seven day full refund from your first live lesson.

06.Who teaches?

Native Italian teachers holding the DITALS qualification for teaching Italian as a foreign language, each in their own city.
