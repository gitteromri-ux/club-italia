The full curriculum

Six live units. One journey.

A structured Cultural · 6 lessons curriculum. Every h4 in Italian is the real Italian topic of the unit, taught live by Alessandro from Milano.

01

Il libretto: leggere l'opera in italiano

Read an opera libretto in real Italian. Understand recitative and aria markings, stage directions, and the poetic Italian of Da Ponte, Boito and Piave. Andante, largo, aria, cavatina, cabaletta, duetto, coro, finale.

02

Verdi: La Traviata al Teatro alla Scala

Enter Verdi's most performed opera in Italian. Libiamo ne' lieti calici, Sempre libera, Amami Alfredo, il finale d'atto secondo, il dramma di Violetta, la Milano borghese dell'Ottocento.

03

Puccini: La Boheme, l'amore e Parigi

Puccini's poetic realism in Italian. Che gelida manina, Mi chiamano Mimi, Quando m'en vo', il quadro terzo alla Barriera d'Enfer, il finale straziante, il verismo pucciniano.

04

Rossini: Il Barbiere di Siviglia

Rossini's comic genius, sung in Italian. Largo al factotum della citta, Una voce poco fa, il crescendo rossiniano, il patter, l'aria del sorbetto, il buffo napoletano, la brillantezza del canto.

05

L'italiano operistico vs l'italiano moderno

The linguistic bridge between operatic and modern Italian. Le forme arcaiche (ei, ella, avvi), le inversioni, le desinenze, il perche di certe scelte poetiche, come Verdi e Puccini hanno modernizzato la lingua del melodramma.

06

Andare a un concerto in italiano: alla Scala di Milano

Attend a concert or opera at La Scala, entirely in Italian. Il palchetto, la platea, il loggione, il programma di sala, l'intervallo, il fuoriprogramma, i bravo e i bis, gli applausi finali, l'atmosfera del gran teatro milanese.

Questions

Everything you might be wondering

Is this really live?＋
Every session is a live class taught by a native teacher in Italy, at the scheduled hour, in a group of ten to twelve. If you cannot attend live, the class is recorded for you to review the same evening.
How many units in the course?＋
6 live units, 85 minutes each. Every unit is filmed live from Italy and is broadcast on the same day, at the same time each week.
What if I have never learned Italian before?＋
If our advisor places you in this course, it is the right starting point for your level. If you are not sure, book a free placement call and we will listen to your Italian for ten minutes and give you an honest answer.
Who is the teacher?＋
Alessandro Ferri, native Italian teacher from Milano. Every unit of this course is taught by Alessandro personally, from the same city, broadcasting live to your small group of ten to twelve.
Will I get a certificate?＋
Yes. On completion you receive a CEFR-aligned certificate issued by eTeacher confirming the target level, plus lifetime access to every recording and one hundred dollars in learning credits.
What comes next after this course?＋
Your teacher will personally recommend your next step. Most learners continue into the next CEFR level in our ladder; some pause and take a Culture Capsule to consolidate. Nothing is automatic.
