01

Twenty regions, twenty countries

Italy contains more variety per square kilometre than any country in Europe. The German-inflected German of Bolzano, the Occitan of the western Alps, the Greek of Calabria, the Arabic-Norman-Spanish palimpsest of Sicily. To travel Italy is to travel a small continent.

02

The five journeys we teach through

Rome as beginning. Florence as language cradle. Bologna as food-and-university heart. Venice as merchant republic. Naples-to-Palermo as southern lyric. Each Club Italia course is set in one of these worlds.

03

Practising your Italian in Italy

The best language school in the world is a two-week trip to Italy after twenty lessons. Every Club Italia alumnus receives a travel-Italian survival kit built from what they learned in class.
