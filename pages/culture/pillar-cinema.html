A cinema that changed cinema

Neorealism in the 1940s. Fellini in the 1960s. Visconti's operatic tragedies. Sorrentino today. Italian cinema, more than any other national cinema in Europe, still gives the world films people carry with them for the rest of their lives.

02

The fashion revolution

Armani, Prada, Valentino, Versace, Ferragamo, Missoni. In one lifetime, Italy went from post-war rubble to dressing the entire luxury world. The language of Italian fashion is now a global lingua franca.

03

What you can watch after your first course

By the end of CI Elementare you can follow La Vita è Bella. By CI Intermedio, Cinema Paradiso and Il Postino. By CI Avanzato, La Grande Bellezza without subtitles.
