Verdi, Puccini, Rossini

Opera & Musica

The most musical language in Europe.

01

The art form born in Italian

Opera was invented in Florence around 1600 and has been sung in Italian ever since. Not because the world hasn't tried other languages — because Italian, with its open vowels and rolling consonants, was engineered for singing.

02

The three composers who shaped a nation

Rossini's comedy, Verdi's tragedy, Puccini's tenderness. Between them they wrote most of the operas anyone still stages. To read a Verdi libretto in Italian is to hear the language a Roman poet gave to a national art form.

03

From La Scala to your living room

At Club Italia the Capsula Culturale L'Opera walks you through six real libretti — Il Barbiere, La Traviata, Aida, La Bohème, Turandot, and Falstaff — so that the next time you attend, you understand every word.
