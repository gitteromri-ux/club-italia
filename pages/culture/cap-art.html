Learning Outcomes

On successful completion,
the learner will be able to.

The following outcomes reference the CEFR descriptors for the Cultural · Any level band and constitute the assessed criteria for course completion.

  1. Read a full Italian museum wall label without translation.
  2. Understand a Uffizi or Vatican Museums audio guide.
  3. Talk about painting, sculpture and architecture in Italian.
  4. Grasp the terminology of the Italian Renaissance.
  5. Discuss Caravaggio, Michelangelo, Raphael and Bernini in their own language.
  6. Take an Italian guided tour with confidence.

The Live Classroom

Your Monday 7pm ET class
with Chiara.

Firenze

Chiara,
signature teacher, L'Arte — Renaissance in the Words That Made It.

Chiara was trained in the Università per Stranieri di Perugia and has taught adult learners at Club Italia since 2019. Before joining the school, Chiara taught in-country for the Firenze branch of the Società Dante Alighieri, the national network of Italian cultural institutes, and served as an examiner for the CELI proficiency examinations issued by the Università per Stranieri di Perugia.

In the classroom, Chiara works on the principle that grammar lives inside dialogue. Every unit is opened by a real recorded exchange between Italians; the grammar point that governs it is drawn out only after learners have already tried the exchange themselves. This is the Club Italia house method and Chiara has been one of its early architects.

Outside of teaching, Chiara writes for the Club Italia culture library on the language of Firenze and its region.

Questions About CAP-AR

Frequently asked.

Who is CAP-AR designed for?

Adult learners approaching Italian at the Cultural · Any level band of the CEFR. There is no prerequisite beyond a 20-minute placement call with a Club Italia advisor.

How long does CAP-AR take?

Six weeks, at one live class per week, plus 10 to 15 recommended study hours over the full course.

Which certification will I receive?

On successful completion of CAP-AR you will sit a short oral assessment. A signed, CEFR-aligned certificate is issued within seven days by Club Italia by eTeacher and references the Cultural · Any level descriptor.

What if I have to miss a live class?

Every live class is recorded and available on your dashboard within 30 minutes. Your teacher will also post a written recap in the weekly homework thread.

What technology do I need?

A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge), a working webcam and microphone, and a stable internet connection of at least 5 Mbps. There is no app to install.

Can I try CAP-AR before enrolling?

Yes. You are welcome to sit an open sample class, free of charge, before you enrol. The Club Italia advisor will suggest the closest upcoming session on your placement call.
