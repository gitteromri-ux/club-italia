The full curriculum

Six live units. One journey.

A structured Cultural · 6 lessons curriculum. Every h4 in Italian is the real Italian topic of the unit, taught live by Chiara from Firenze.

01

Il vocabolario del Rinascimento

The essential lexicon of Renaissance art in Italian. La bottega, l'apprendista, la commissione, il mecenate, il fondo oro, la tempera, l'affresco, la prospettiva, il chiaroscuro, il disegno preparatorio.

02

Michelangelo e Vasari raccontati in italiano

Read Vasari's Vite in Italian, with Chiara guiding you through the story of Michelangelo. Il divino Michelangelo, la Cappella Sistina, il David di Firenze, la Pieta vaticana, il non finito, il rapporto con Giulio II.

03

La prospettiva: da Brunelleschi a Piero della Francesca

The invention that changed Western art, explained in Italian. Il punto di fuga, la linea d'orizzonte, la costruzione geometrica, la tavoletta di Brunelleschi, la Flagellazione di Urbino, la profondita illusoria.

04

Affresco vs olio su tela: le tecniche

The two great techniques of Italian painting. L'affresco, l'intonaco fresco, la giornata di lavoro, la pittura a buon fresco. L'olio su tela, la preparazione, gli strati, le velature, i tempi di essiccazione.

05

Firenze e i Medici: il potere e l'arte

The Medici and the making of Florence, in Italian. Cosimo il Vecchio, Lorenzo il Magnifico, il mecenatismo, la corte medicea, gli Uffizi, la cacciata dei Medici, il ritorno, il Granducato di Toscana.

06

L'arte italiana contemporanea

From Futurismo to today's biennale, in Italian. Il Futurismo di Marinetti, la Metafisica di De Chirico, la Transavanguardia di Achille Bonito Oliva, l'Arte Povera, la Biennale di Venezia, i grandi collezionisti italiani.

Questions

Everything you might be wondering

Is this really live?＋
Every session is a live class taught by a native teacher in Italy, at the scheduled hour, in a group of ten to twelve. If you cannot attend live, the class is recorded for you to review the same evening.
How many units in the course?＋
6 live units, 85 minutes each. Every unit is filmed live from Italy and is broadcast on the same day, at the same time each week.
What if I have never learned Italian before?＋
If our advisor places you in this course, it is the right starting point for your level. If you are not sure, book a free placement call and we will listen to your Italian for ten minutes and give you an honest answer.
Who is the teacher?＋
Chiara Ferretti, native Italian teacher from Firenze. Every unit of this course is taught by Chiara personally, from the same city, broadcasting live to your small group of ten to twelve.
Will I get a certificate?＋
Yes. On completion you receive a CEFR-aligned certificate issued by eTeacher confirming the target level, plus lifetime access to every recording and one hundred dollars in learning credits.
What comes next after this course?＋
Your teacher will personally recommend your next step. Most learners continue into the next CEFR level in our ladder; some pause and take a Culture Capsule to consolidate. Nothing is automatic.
