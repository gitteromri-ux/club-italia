Inside a single lesson

Eighty-five minutes with Alessandro.

Lesson 07 · Parlare di lavoro. Every learner logs in at 8pm EST on a Tuesday. The camera opens on Alessandro's desk in Milano. This is what the next eighty-five minutes look like.

Learning goals

  • You can hold the scene of the lesson in Italian for four full turns.
  • You can use the new grammar without translating in your head.
  • You can pronounce the new vocabulary the way Alessandro does.
  • You leave with two written examples in your own notebook.

The 85-minute flow

  1. 00:00 to 00:10 · Warm greetings and last week's carryover.
  2. 00:10 to 00:30 · The new scene, first read together, then acted out in pairs.
  3. 00:30 to 00:50 · Grammar in the moment. Alessandro draws on the whiteboard.
  4. 00:50 to 01:10 · Pair rooms with focused correction from Alessandro.
  5. 01:10 to 01:25 · Group reunion, final rehearsal, homework for next week.

Between-lesson work

  • A twelve-minute Biagio conversation on the new scene.
  • One short reading, one short listening, one short writing.
  • Optional: a matching cultural clip from our library.
  • Expected weekly load: about ninety minutes outside class.

The city is the classroom

Milan, the city that made Italian modern.

Milan is the Italy of the twenty-first century. It is the city of Prada, La Scala, the aperitivo hour and the Corriere della Sera. The Italian spoken here is fast, precise, and unafraid of an English loanword when it earns its place.

Alessandro teaches from an apartment near Corso Como, and he is our opera scholar as well as our northern Italian specialist. His lessons close the CEFR arc by taking you into contemporary Italian, the language of a newspaper editorial and a boardroom.

You will leave the term ready to sit an exam, to accept a job offer, or to hold your own with the fastest speakers in the country.

Milano, Italy

Alessandro has taught Italian to adult learners for over a decade. She holds a full degree in Italian language and literature and a recognised international teaching certification.

Her lessons are built on scenes, not slides. She believes an adult learner deserves the same respect a graduate student receives in a Perugia or Siena classroom, and she plans every ninety minutes on that principle.

She teaches only from Milano. When you take her class, you take it into the country she loves and knows.

Common questions

Everything worth asking.

Is Chiacchierando really live?＋
Every one of the 20 lessons is a live session on Zoom with Alessandro Conti in Milano. Nothing is a recording, nothing is automated. If you cannot attend, the lesson is recorded for you to watch the same night.
What if my Italian is too weak for Parliamo · Confident?＋
Your placement call with an advisor will confirm that Parliamo · Confident is the right stage for you. If it is not, we will recommend the correct stage without pressure.
Who teaches Chiacchierando?＋
Alessandro Conti teaches every group of this course from Milano. She holds Bocconi & Cattolica and has taught adult learners for over a decade.
What time zone does the class run in?＋
This course is taught in evening slots convenient for the Americas and morning slots convenient for Europe and Asia. Your placement call will confirm the exact group time.
What if I miss a class?＋
The class is recorded and available to you the same evening. Your teacher will also send you a short personal note so you never fall behind.

Yes. The course ends with a formal oral and written CEFR Spoken A2 → B1 assessment. On successful completion you receive a printed certificate.
