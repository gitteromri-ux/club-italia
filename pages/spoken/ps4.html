What you will be able to do

By the last lesson, you can:

«Understands the main points of clear speech on familiar matters, handles most travel situations and explains opinions and plans.»

CEFR B1, Council of Europe

  • Hold a full 30-minute Italian conversation on any everyday topic
  • Express opinion, doubt, sarcasm and humour
  • Disagree politely and change someone's mind
  • Tell a story with structure, pacing and punchline
  • Understand rapid native banter and jokes
  • Sit and pass an internationally recognised B1 oral exam
Syllabus

20 units. One clear arc.

  1. 01

    Chiacchierare, l'arte italiana

    What chiacchierare really means. The philosophy of unhurried talk.

  2. 02

    Raccontare una storia

    Storytelling structure, introduction, complication, punchline. Italian narrative style.

  3. 03

    Fare una battuta

    Italian humour, the setup, the beat, the payoff. Regional humour.

  4. 04

    Discutere di calcio

    Football arguments. Fifteen minutes on Juve vs Milan and no one leaves offended.

  5. 05

    Discutere di politica

    Political conversation, how to hold a strong opinion without breaking a friendship.

  6. 06

    Parlare di famiglia

    Family talk, the mother-in-law joke, the cousin gossip, the Sunday lunch.

  7. 07

    Parlare di lavoro

    Work talk, complaining, bragging, gossiping about the boss.

  8. 08

    Parlare di soldi

    Money talk, Italy's ambiguity around money. What is said and what is meant.

  9. 09

    Parlare di amore

    Love talk, flirting, breaking up, staying friends. The vocabulary of the heart.

  10. 10

    Metà strada

    Full midpoint conversation lab, 30-minute unstructured chat with the teacher.

  11. 11

    Il pettegolezzo

    Gossip Italian, the register, the euphemisms, the raised eyebrow.

  12. 12

    La lamentela

    The Italian art of complaining, bureaucratic, personal, existential.

  13. 13

    Il consiglio

    Giving advice in Italian, what to say, what to never say.

  14. 14

    La discussione

    Real disagreement, how to argue and still finish the meal together.

  15. 15

    La riconciliazione

    Making up in Italian, the words, the gestures, the coffee that follows.

  16. 16

    Il monologo comico

    Stand-up Italian, analyzing a comedian's routine and trying one yourself.

  17. 17

    Il talk show

    Watching a real Italian talk show together. Register, interrupting, humour.

  18. 18

    Il podcast

    Listening to a real Italian podcast, then discussing it in Italian for 40 minutes.

  19. 19

    L'intervista

    Being interviewed and interviewing, a full role-play in Italian.

  20. 20

    Arrivederci, al livello B1

    Final CEFR B1 oral assessment, graduation ceremony, path to advanced study.
