The full curriculum

20 units. One clear arc.

Every lesson has a named objective, a grammar or language focus and a setting. Open a unit to read it.

01Alla stazioneLesson 1 · 85 min

Buying a ticket at Termini, Trenitalia vs Italo, first vs second class.

02In trenoLesson 2 · 85 min

Italian train vocabulary. Coincidence, ritardo, il capotreno.

03All'aeroportoLesson 3 · 85 min

Check-in, security, boarding, delays, Italian airport register.

04In taxiLesson 4 · 85 min

Taxi Italian, meter, tipping, negotiating an out-of-town ride.

05In albergoLesson 5 · 85 min

Hotel check-in and check-out. Requesting a room change, room service, breakfast.

06Al B&BLesson 6 · 85 min

Bed & breakfast Italian, meeting the host, house rules, breakfast conversations.

07In AirbnbLesson 7 · 85 min

Renting a private apartment, codes, keys, house manuals, neighbour relations.

08Al museoLesson 8 · 85 min

Museum Italian, audio guide, guided tour, understanding a wall label.

09Alla mostraLesson 9 · 85 min

Exhibition Italian, talking to a curator, buying the catalogue, opinion.

10Metà stradaLesson 10 · 85 min

Midpoint full oral assessment set on a virtual Italian trip.

11In farmaciaLesson 11 · 85 min

Pharmacy Italian, describing symptoms, over-the-counter medicine, prescriptions.

12Dal medicoLesson 12 · 85 min

At the doctor, the appointment, the visit, the referral.

13Al pronto soccorsoLesson 13 · 85 min

Emergency room Italian, the codes, the triage, the paperwork.

14In bancaLesson 14 · 85 min

Bank Italian, changing money, opening an account, ATM problems.

15Alla postaLesson 15 · 85 min

Post office Italian, sending a package, paying a bill, buying stamps.

16Al ristorante in autostradaLesson 16 · 85 min

Autogrill Italian, the fast lunch on the motorway, ordering at the counter.

17In montagnaLesson 17 · 85 min

Mountain Italian, rifugio, sentiero, guida alpina, mal di montagna.

18Al mareLesson 18 · 85 min

Beach Italian, lettino, ombrellone, bagnino, cabina.

19In cittàLesson 19 · 85 min

Urban Italian, public transport, ZTL, parking, the metro in Rome, Milan, Naples.

20Arrivederci, e buon viaggioLesson 20 · 85 min

Final oral, cultural graduation, path to Chiacchierando.

Your teacher · Venezia

Francesca Zeno

Native teacher · DITALS II

Francesca is Venetian. She grew up in the Cannaregio neighbourhood, took a first degree at Ca' Foscari, then trained specifically in the Italian of tourism and cultural travel. She teaches from an apartment overlooking a side canal. Her students often say the light behind her on the camera is enough on its own.

«Travel Italian is often taught badly. It is taught as a list of phrases you memorise and then forget the moment a real Italian speaks back to you. I teach it as language for a specific trip.»

Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

01.Are the classes live?

Every class is live, taught by a native teacher broadcasting from Italy. Recordings are for revision, never a substitute.

02.How big are the groups?

Ten to twelve adults. Everyone speaks in every lesson and the teacher corrects each learner personally.

03.What does a course include?

Twenty live lessons of 85 minutes (six sessions of 60 minutes for a capsule), the PDF syllabus, recordings, Biagio the AI tutor and a CEFR-mapped Club Italia certificate.

04.Do you prepare for CILS and CELI?

Yes, from A2 upward. You sit the exam at an authorised centre of the Università per Stranieri di Siena (CILS) or di Perugia (CELI). Club Italia is not an exam centre.

05.How much does it cost?

From $62 a week on the annual plan, $73 a week by term, $84 a week monthly. Seven day full refund from your first live lesson.

06.Who teaches?

Native Italian teachers holding the DITALS qualification for teaching Italian as a foreign language, each in their own city.
