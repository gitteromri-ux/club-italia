What you will be able to do

By the last lesson, you can:

«Understands and uses familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases. Introduces themselves and interacts simply.»

CEFR A1, Council of Europe

  • Order coffee, food and drinks with cultural confidence
  • Small-talk with a barista, a neighbour or a taxi driver
  • Understand the fast, warm rhythm of café Italian
  • Handle greetings, farewells and social pleasantries
  • React naturally with typical Italian interjections
  • Speak Italian without stopping to translate in your head
Syllabus

20 units. One clear arc.

  1. 01

    Buongiorno, un caffè

    The espresso ritual, every word that happens at a Roman bar.

  2. 02

    Al banco

    Standing at the bar vs sitting at the table. Prices, timing, tipping.

  3. 03

    Il barista

    Meeting the same barista every morning. The Italian art of daily kindness.

  4. 04

    Il vicino di banco

    Small talk with the person next to you at the bar.

  5. 05

    Il tempo

    Weather chit-chat, the second-most-Italian conversation.

  6. 06

    La squadra

    Football at the bar. Every man and woman in Italy has an opinion.

  7. 07

    La politica

    Politics at the bar, how to nod, agree, and not commit.

  8. 08

    Il turista

    When you are addressed in English, how to reply, gently, in Italian.

  9. 09

    Il cornetto

    Ordering breakfast. Sweet vs savoury, cream vs jam, the difference between un cornetto and un croissant.

  10. 10

    Metà strada

    Midpoint conversation lab, a full 15-minute unstructured chat with the teacher.

  11. 11

    Il pranzo veloce

    Ordering a quick lunch, panino, tramezzino, insalatona.

  12. 12

    L'aperitivo

    The Milanese ritual, spritz, negroni, and the vocabulary of small plates.

  13. 13

    Il conto

    Paying the bill. Coperto, servizio, mancia. Splitting the bill Italian-style.

  14. 14

    Il caffè da asporto

    Take-away Italian, a very recent phenomenon that has its own new vocabulary.

  15. 15

    La colazione della domenica

    Sunday breakfast at a bar, bombolone, sfogliatella, maritozzo.

  16. 16

    Il caffè in ufficio

    Office caffè culture, the coffee run, the machine, the coffee gossip.

  17. 17

    Il caffè in famiglia

    Grandmother's moka pot. The most sacred coffee of all.

  18. 18

    Il caffè in albergo

    Hotel breakfast Italian, buffet vs à la carte, the polite requests.

  19. 19

    Il caffè al mare

    Summer at the beach bar. Granita, gelato, spremuta d'arancia.

  20. 20

    Arrivederci, e a presto

    Final oral conversation, cultural graduation, path to A Tavola.
