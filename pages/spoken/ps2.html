What you will learn

CEFR target · A2

«Can understand sentences and frequently used expressions related to areas of immediate relevance and communicate in simple, routine tasks.»

Global descriptor, paraphrased from the Council of Europe CEFR.

Can-do outcomes

  • Read any Italian menu without pointing
  • Ask for a table, a wine, a dish, and a change, politely
  • Understand a waiter's rapid recitation of the specials
  • Argue politely about a dish or a bill
  • Talk about food history, region and cuisine
  • Cook and talk in Italian at the same time
The full curriculum

20 units. One clear arc.

Every lesson has a named objective, a grammar or language focus and a setting. Open a unit to read it.

01Il menùLesson 1 · 85 min

Reading a full Italian menu: antipasto, primo, secondo, contorno, dolce.

02La prenotazioneLesson 2 · 85 min

Booking a table by phone. Time, number of people, dietary needs.

03Il vinoLesson 3 · 85 min

Ordering wine. Regional grape varieties, house wine vs bottle, the sommelier.

04Gli antipastiLesson 4 · 85 min

Bruschetta, salumi, formaggi, Italy's opening course in every region.

05I primiLesson 5 · 85 min

Pasta shapes and their sauces. The rules that Italians never break.

06I secondiLesson 6 · 85 min

Meat, fish, vegetarian, contorno pairing. Regional traditions.

07Il dolce e il caffèLesson 7 · 85 min

Tiramisù, panna cotta, cannolo, and the sacred espresso after.

08L'osteLesson 8 · 85 min

Talking to the owner. The Italian art of building a relationship with the trattoria.

09Al mercatoLesson 9 · 85 min

Buying at the market, asking the fishmonger, the greengrocer, the butcher.

10Metà stradaLesson 10 · 85 min

Midpoint full conversational assessment set in a virtual trattoria.

11La cucina di casaLesson 11 · 85 min

Home cooking Italian, la nonna, la mamma, la ricetta di famiglia.

12Cucinare insiemeLesson 12 · 85 min

Cooking together in real time, verbs of preparation, quantities, timings.

13La pizzaLesson 13 · 85 min

Ordering a pizza. Neapolitan vs Roman vs alla pala. The rules of margherita.

14Il gelatoLesson 14 · 85 min

Ordering gelato, sizes, flavours, cono vs coppa, panna sì o no.

15La sagraLesson 15 · 85 min

Italian food festivals, the sagra del tartufo, della porchetta, dell'oliva.

16Il ristorante di lussoLesson 16 · 85 min

High-end restaurant Italian. Michelin-star register, degustazione.

17La trattoria di quartiereLesson 17 · 85 min

Neighbourhood trattoria Italian, the friendly regular.

18Le regole non scritteLesson 18 · 85 min

Cappuccino after 11 am? Never. Parmigiano on seafood? Never. The rules.

19Il contoLesson 19 · 85 min

Paying the bill. Splitting Italian-style, tipping, the ricevuta fiscale.

20Arrivederci, a tavolaLesson 20 · 85 min

Final oral, cultural graduation, path to In Viaggio.

Your teacher · Firenze

Chiara Bellini

Native teacher · DITALS II

Chiara was born in Fiesole, above Florence, and grew up walking down the hill to school past cypress rows that appear in half the paintings you have already seen. She read literature at Firenze, taught in Barcelona for two years, then came home. Her Italian is standard Tuscan, the source Italian, the one from which the language on television and in newspapers is drawn. Her English is warm and careful and does not compete with the Italian for space in the class.

«I teach adults. Adults come with a life already written in another language, and my job is not to erase it. My job is to give them a second interior voice, one that speaks Italian back to them when they are washing dishes or driving home.»

Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

01.Are the classes live?

Every class is live, taught by a native teacher broadcasting from Italy. Recordings are for revision, never a substitute.

02.How big are the groups?

Ten to twelve adults. Everyone speaks in every lesson and the teacher corrects each learner personally.

03.What does a course include?

Twenty live lessons of 85 minutes (six sessions of 60 minutes for a capsule), the PDF syllabus, recordings, Biagio the AI tutor and a CEFR-mapped Club Italia certificate.

04.Do you prepare for CILS and CELI?

Yes, from A2 upward. You sit the exam at an authorised centre of the Università per Stranieri di Siena (CILS) or di Perugia (CELI). Club Italia is not an exam centre.

05.How much does it cost?

From $62 a week on the annual plan, $73 a week by term, $84 a week monthly. Seven day full refund from your first live lesson.

06.Who teaches?

Native Italian teachers holding the DITALS qualification for teaching Italian as a foreign language, each in their own city.
