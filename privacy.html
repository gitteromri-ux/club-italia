1. Who we are

Club Italia is a trading style of eTeacher Group Ltd, incorporated in Israel with additional operations in the United States and the United Kingdom. This privacy notice governs clubitalia.live and every enrolment, communication, and payment channel we operate under the Club Italia name.

2. What we collect

We collect the personal data you give us directly: name, email, phone, timezone, self-assessed Italian level, billing address, and payment method on enrolment.

We collect technical data automatically via cookies: IP address, device, browser, session length, and the pages you visit — solely for site performance and product analytics.

Inside the live classroom we record your video and audio so absent classmates can watch and so you can review your own lesson later. Recordings are stored for the life of your account and can be deleted on request.

3. How we use it

To reply to your inquiry, deliver your Italian course, issue your CEFR certificate, provide customer and academic support, improve the platform, and, with your explicit opt-in, send you occasional cultural essays and course news.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertising networks. We do not use your video or audio to train any AI model without your explicit written consent.

4. Legal basis

Contract performance for course delivery. Legitimate interest for platform analytics. Explicit consent for marketing communications and for any use of recorded video or audio beyond internal course delivery.

5. Who we share it with

Our payment processor (Stripe), our email delivery provider (SendGrid), our live-video platform (Zoom for Education), and our analytics provider (Plausible, cookieless). Every processor is contractually bound to protect your data and to hold it only as long as required to deliver the service.

6. International transfers

Data may be transferred between Israel, the United States, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. All transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards recognised by the receiving jurisdiction.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, port, restrict, object to, and delete your personal data at any time. Email advisor@eTeacherGroup.com and we will respond within thirty days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Retention

Enrolment and course records: seven years after your last course, for tax and academic-record obligations. Marketing consents: until you withdraw them. Analytics data: thirteen months, aggregated. Video recordings: for the life of your account, deletable on request.

9. Security

Encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Access to personal data is restricted to named staff. All staff pass annual data-protection training. We publish a security page separately and disclose any material breach within seventy-two hours.

10. Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies for login and session. Optional analytics cookies (Plausible, cookieless by default). No third-party advertising cookies. Full cookie preferences are managed in your account dashboard.

11. Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. Material changes will be announced by email to registered learners at least thirty days before they take effect.

12. Contact

Data Protection Officer: dpo@eTeacherGroup.com. General inquiries: advisor@eTeacherGroup.com. Postal: eTeacher Group Ltd, Herzliya, Israel.

Questions or requests: advisor@eTeacherGroup.com. We respond within one business day.
