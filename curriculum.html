A curriculum written against the framework.

The Common European Framework describes six levels by what a learner can do. Every Club Italia unit has a stated objective, a grammar focus, vocabulary and a cultural setting, and moves you one step along those descriptors.

    The full curriculum

    Six levels

    The syllabus, level by level.

    Global descriptors paraphrased from the Council of Europe CEFR (2001, Companion Volume 2020). Skill statements follow the CEFR self-assessment grid. Grammar progression follows common Italian L2 practice, cf. the Profilo della lingua italiana (Università per Stranieri di Perugia).

    A1Breakthrough Basic userCI Principiante · CI Elementare · Al Caffè

    «Can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases, introduce themselves and others, and interact simply if the other person speaks slowly.»

    SkillAt A1 you can…
    ListeningFamiliar words and very basic phrases about self, family and surroundings.
    ReadingNames, notices, simple forms and menus.
    Spoken interactionAsk and answer simple questions on immediate needs.
    Spoken productionDescribe where you live and people you know.
    WritingA postcard, a form with personal details.

    Grammar focus. Essere and avere, regular presente, articles and gender, plural nouns, numbers, c'è / ci sono, basic prepositions, piacere.

    See the CI Principiante syllabus

    A2Waystage Basic userCI Intermedio · CI Avanzato · A Tavola · In Viaggio

    «Can understand frequently used expressions about immediate areas of relevance, communicate in simple routine tasks and describe their background and immediate needs.»

    SkillAt A2 you can…
    ListeningShort clear announcements and the main point of simple messages.
    ReadingShort simple texts, ads, timetables, personal letters.
    Spoken interactionShort social exchanges, shopping, travel, bookings.
    Spoken productionDescribe family, education, routine, past events.
    WritingShort notes and simple personal letters.

    Grammar focus. Passato prossimo with essere and avere, imperfetto, reflexive verbs, direct and indirect object pronouns, futuro semplice, comparatives, ci and ne.

    See the CI Intermedio syllabus

    B1Threshold Independent userChiacchierando · CILS / CELI B1 preparation

    «Can understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar matters, deal with most travel situations, describe experiences and give brief reasons for opinions and plans.»

    SkillAt B1 you can…
    ListeningMain points of radio, TV and talk on familiar topics.
    ReadingEveryday texts, descriptions of events and feelings.
    Spoken interactionEnter unprepared conversation on familiar topics.
    Spoken productionNarrate a story, a plot, an experience, with reasons.
    WritingConnected text on familiar topics, personal letters.

    Grammar focus. Condizionale presente, congiuntivo presente, pronomi combinati, trapassato prossimo, relative pronouns, imperative with pronouns.

    See the Chiacchierando syllabus

    B2Vantage Independent userAdvanced cohorts on demand

    «Can understand the main ideas of complex text, interact with fluency and spontaneity, and produce clear detailed text, explaining a viewpoint.»

    SkillAt B2 you can…
    ListeningExtended speech and lectures, most TV news.
    ReadingArticles and reports with a stance; contemporary prose.
    Spoken interactionFluent interaction with native speakers; sustain views.
    Spoken productionClear detailed descriptions; pros and cons.
    WritingEssays and reports, argued.

    Grammar focus. Congiuntivo imperfetto and trapassato, periodo ipotetico (all types), passive voice and si passivante, gerundio, connectives.

    C1Effective operational proficiency Proficient userAdvanced cohorts on demand

    «Can understand demanding, longer texts, express ideas fluently and spontaneously, and use language flexibly for social, academic and professional purposes.»

    SkillAt C1 you can…
    ListeningExtended speech even when not clearly structured.
    ReadingLong complex factual and literary texts; specialised articles.
    Spoken interactionExpress yourself fluently without searching for words.
    Spoken productionDetailed descriptions of complex subjects.
    WritingWell-structured text on complex subjects; style choices.

    Grammar focus. Indirect speech and concordanza dei tempi, register and formality, nominalisation, advanced connectives, passato remoto in reading.

    C2Mastery Proficient userAdvanced cohorts on demand

    «Can understand virtually everything heard or read, summarise from different sources and express themselves spontaneously and precisely, differentiating finer shades of meaning.»

    SkillAt C2 you can…
    ListeningAny kind of spoken language, live or broadcast, at native speed.
    ReadingVirtually all forms of written language.
    Spoken interactionIdiomatic, precise, effortless.
    Spoken productionClear, smoothly flowing, well-structured argument.
    WritingComplex letters, reports, reviews of literary works.

    Grammar focus. Stylistic and regional variation, literary tenses, idiom and collocation, rhetoric.

    Anatomy of a unit

    Every lesson, five parts.

    01

    Objective

    One can-do statement, written on the shared screen at minute one.

    02

    Setting

    A real Italian place: a bar, a market, a station, a workshop.

    03

    Language focus

    The grammar and vocabulary the task needs, and no more.

    04

    Task

    Pairs and trios in breakout rooms, then back to the group.

    Fifth: the can-do check. You leave each lesson able to do one thing in Italian you could not do before.
